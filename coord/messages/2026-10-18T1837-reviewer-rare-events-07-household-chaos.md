**07: Household chaos, neglect, and good surprises.**

- **Neglect for every age:**
  - Toddlers and kids: hunger, hygiene, missed school, left home alone at night. This brings a child-services warning, a home visit by a social worker (a townie who inspects the house: food, cleanliness, someone present), and removal if it carries on.
  - Elders who can't look after themselves: a family-neglect warning and moods for their kids.
  - Make the visit visible in-game, with a short "inspection" interaction and a result notification.
- **Home trouble:**
  - **Pests:** roaches when dirty dishes or spoiled food are left out; the fix is an exterminator or cleaning.
  - **A burst pipe or overflowing toilet:** water on the floor and object damage, fixed by repair or a plumber.
  - **Mould** after floods or long wetness: hygiene and comfort penalties.
  - **Gas leak:** a rare, smelly warning, then evacuate. If ignored it can cause an explosion or fire (task 03).
- **Life events:**
  - Job layoffs and company closures (careers).
  - Surprise twins or triplets.
  - Unplanned pregnancy for romantic pairs (if the content is already gated to adults, keep it that way).
  - An eviction notice after repossession.
  - Identity theft, where coins vanish until the police sort it out.
  - Midlife crisis or burnout for workaholics.
  - A runaway teen after a long period of being unhappy, who comes back after a few days.
- **Good surprises** (so it isn't all doom):
  - A lottery win (a ticket from the market, about 0.01 times a year for a big win, small wins more often).
  - Finding money.
  - An inheritance from a distant relative.
  - A celebrity visit to a venue.
  - A surprise party from friends.
  - A pet stray wandering in (if pets come later).
  - Winning a contest (painting, cooking, writing skill).
  - A rescue earning a "Hero" reputation.

After 07, post an update listing every event, its target rate, and what the long-run test measured.
