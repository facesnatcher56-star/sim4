**14b done: residents use the places by themselves** (branch agent/life14b, not pushed; merged origin/main first).

What it does: every resident (an unplayed household, and the player's own folk while they are on autonomy; someone with a player order in the queue is never pulled away) plans a few outings a day, weighted by traits, needs, money, time of day and a partner, and goes there with the SAME `LifeCommand` calls the Town window makes (`go`, `use`, `phone_invite`, `phone_respond`, `college_apply`, `home`). Kids: preschool for toddlers, school as before, a homework buff so they sit down at a desk, the homework club. Adults: doctor when ill (hourly check), dentist (a sore tooth at once), counselling after grief, check-up/flu shot, prescription refills; restaurant, burgers, bakery, ice cream, food truck, cinema, plays, concerts, museum, gallery, bowling, arcade, pool, lake, pick-up games, gym, league, clubs, bar and nightclub (adults only by the place's own rule); pets fed/walked/played with hourly by whoever is home, vet trips; phone invitations answered; weddings, birthday parties, baby showers, funerals, reunions hosted by the person they concern; a late teen applies to college; the ambitious take lectures and night classes. Townies: one dice a day (a counted visit). Data: `data/life/95_auto.json`; code `PlaceAuto*` (7 small files).

Tests: `life_auto_test` (fast, 8 s, 10 groups), `life_auto_longrun_test` (slow, 28 days, 3 households, ~8 min), `life_auto_home_longrun_test` (slow, real map, 5 days, off vs on). Numbers and what is not covered: docs/LIFE.md "Residents on their own (task 14b)".

Hash: `tests/baseline/coop_hash_3day.json` regenerated on purpose (main's 3f1f0752... -> 37e06aa2...): the 3-day Hood run now includes outings. Checked first that with `config.auto.enabled` false the old hash is reproduced exactly, so the feature is the only change.

Small hooks in shared files: `PlaceUse` (opts `quiet`, `auto`), `LifeOuting.go` / `PhoneCmd.invite` / `respond` (optional `use_opts`), `NeighborhoodTravel` + `NeighborhoodLots` (opts `silent`: no head-to/arrived toast for unplayed households), `LifeCommand` (`home`, `college_apply`), `EduCollege.apply`, `EduSchool` (the `homework_due` buff), `SimRegistry` (two rows) and `sim_scheduler_test` (the minute row list).
