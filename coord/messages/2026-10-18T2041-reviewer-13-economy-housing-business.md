**13: Economy, housing and businesses.** Second expansion task, after 12. Same rules.

- **A living economy:**
  - Prices for goods, rent and wages drift with the town's prosperity, with booms and recessions (layoffs in 07 tie in).
  - Unemployment rate, inflation and the town budget (12) shown as town stats in the Chronicle and paper.
- **Shops sell real stock:**
  - The market, a new **supermarket**, clothing, furniture, hardware, a pharmacy, a bookshop and an electronics shop.
  - Stock runs out and is restocked, sales and holiday deals, and the buy-mode catalogue partly comes from shops (go and buy it, or order online with delivery).
- **Owning a business** (player or townie):
  - Buy or rent a commercial lot and pick a type: cafe, shop, restaurant, salon, gym, bar or bakery.
  - Set prices and opening hours, hire staff (townies with skills and wages), and keep stock.
  - Customers come by reputation and location. Reviews, profit and loss, and expanding or going bankrupt.
  - Townie businesses run the same way statistically and can compete with yours.
- **Housing market:**
  - **Buy or rent:** mortgages with monthly payments, landlords (a townie or the player), rent increases, eviction for unpaid rent (ties into repossession).
  - **Property values** move with the neighbourhood, crime rate (09c), schools, parks and the house's condition.
  - **Real estate agent:** move to another house or an apartment (10), sell your home, buy a second property and rent it out.
- **Banking and money:**
  - A **bank** building with savings (interest), loans (credit score from your bill history) and debt.
  - Insurance policies (home, health, car, life) that pay out on 03, 05, 06 and deaths.
  - Inheritance through a will (a "Write a will" interaction).
  - Gambling (a lottery, already planned, and optionally card games) with addiction risk.
- **Jobs beyond the 14 careers:**
  - Part-time jobs for teens, freelancing and selling crafts (paintings, books, garden produce, fish) at the market or online, odd jobs, and remote work over the internet (12).
  - Unemployment benefit.
