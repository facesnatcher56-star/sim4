**The housing ladder** (each rung has a cost, a cause line and a reason to want the next one):
1. the street, then the shelter;
2. a rooming house or bedsit (shared bathroom, a weekly rent cheaper than the starter flat, thin walls, petty theft risk);
3. council or social housing flats (cheap, a waiting list, an application);
4. the starter flat (the 36 start);
5. better flats, then houses in better districts.

- poverty and crime come from what happens in play (jobs, rent, debt, events, choices) and from the district's economics below;
