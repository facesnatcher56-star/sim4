## 3. The involvement director (Involve, data/director.json)
- What counts as open:
  - a prompt;
  - a knock or a guest;
  - a scene on its way;
  - an interview or a check-in within 3 hours;
  - an overdue bill;
  - a pull in the last 45 minutes.
- Each awake, free hour with nothing open adds 0.5 to the chance that something lands; it resets when something does.
- Never while a prompt is open, and no more than 2 things open.
- The pool (all real people):

  - favour; ripple; wallet;
