**Task 13 (economy, housing, businesses) is merged to `main`**, code in this same push. Everything is optional for the player and the world runs without them. The whole track is off until `MarketEconomy.start()` (called from `SystemsManager.new_game`; a save without the `econ` block also starts it fresh); while off every multiplier is 1.0 and nothing ticks.

1. **Living economy:** prosperity -1 to 1 with booms and recessions (chronicle notes), slow inflation, prices, rents and wages following prosperity at different speeds, townie layoffs in a recession and hiring in a boom, unemployment rate and a monthly report. `town_budget()` is a stub until task 12.
2. **Shops (`data/shops.json`):** 9 shops as data on existing lots (supermarket, stalls, clothing, pharmacy, hardware on the market; bookshop on the library; furniture and electronics on the office). Stock sells down and restocks daily, weekly sales and holiday deals, online orders add a delivery fee and arrive next day, furniture and electronics become parcels that build mode places free.
3. **Businesses:** 7 types on slots of existing lots; the player rents or buys, sets prices and hours, hires unemployed townies, stocks, expands, sells. Customers come from reputation, price, location, competition and prosperity; reviews, profit and loss, bankruptcy; townies found and run businesses with the same model and compete.
4. **Housing:** 4 map houses plus 5 rotating statistical listings; values from neighbourhood, schools, parks, condition, crime rate and a market index; mortgages (20% down, 40 weekly payments), landlords with rent rises capped at 8% per review, a tenant's rent is an ordinary Economy bill (late fees, eviction notice at the second late rent, three missed mortgage payments foreclose).
5. **Banking:** savings with interest, loans and mortgages in one book, a credit score of 300 to 850, health, car, life insurance beside home cover, "Write a will" (inheritance on `Aging.kill`), lottery tickets, a card game, an addiction track (the `gambling_addiction` event is the Health hook).
6. **Work beyond careers:** teen part-time job, freelance and odd jobs, crafts (balanced below existing income: prices under what the activity pays, per-kind daily demand caps, each sale lowers the price), recession layoffs with severance, unemployment benefit; remote work and internet are stubs until task 12.

Every money movement goes through the Economy ledger with a reason. Every player action goes through `MarketCommand.run(player_id, folk_id, action, args)` (multiplayer-ready rule 1).
