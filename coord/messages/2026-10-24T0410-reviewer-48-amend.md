**48 amendment (owner): don't show how you know them.** "How you know them shouldn't be player-facing text."

- Drop the relationship framing from prompts: no "Mira, your partner of 3 weeks", no "your neighbour", no "an acquaintance", no relationship labels, durations or scores.
- Say only **who** (their name), **what** they ask and **why** it's happening, written the way the person would say it. The relationship can still shape the **wording** behind the scenes (a partner speaks more warmly, a stranger more formally), but it's never stated.
- The same applies to option hints: describe what the answer does ("she'll be grateful, you'll be short this week"), not relationship numbers.
