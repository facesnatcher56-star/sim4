**Owner decision, which changes part 1 of 52: freedom to try, and the world reacts.** In the owner's words: "We should not restrict what the player could do if realistically they could do it, just because it doesn't fit the situation. You're saying they can't kiss because it requires a relationship level. They don't need to know that, and it shouldn't stop them. They could try to kiss a random person, and that person should respond in a realistic way... Things have freedom and the world will react naturally."

So the rule for 52 is now:

**Hide only what is truly impossible right now.**
- Physical or practical impossibility: no ingredients, not your computer, no phone on you, the person is away, asleep behind a locked door, or in a cell.
- Knowledge you don't have. You can't "Talk about fishing" if you've never learned they fish, and you can't "Ask about the trial" if you haven't heard about it. Learned topics still unlock named options as 52 says.

**Social fit never hides an action.**
- Kiss, flirt, hug, prank, insult, confront, deep talk, share a secret, ask out and the rest stay on the menu for strangers and acquaintances too.
- The relationship_above tests on social actions stop being availability gates. They become the input to how the other person reacts.
- No "not right now" or "requires" text for social fit. The player never sees a relationship number or gate.

**The reaction is the consequence.** Build a reaction layer for "out of place" social actions, based on stage, traits, mood, place, who's watching, and history.
- **Range for a stranger kiss:** pulls away shocked, slaps, shoves, yells, laughs it off, is flattered (rare: high romance compatibility, flirty trait, drunk at a bar), or calls the police (harassment, which goes through the existing justice path with a real complaint, witness and report, not a magic charge).
- **Other cases:**
  - a stranger asked for a secret: a brush-off or "who are you?";
  - a deep talk with a stranger: awkward, or a sudden connection;
  - a prank on a stranger: anger, a fight, or a laugh;
  - a hug from someone with a grudge: pushed off.
- **Lasting effects that ripple:** a relationship hit, a grudge or memory, an embarrassed or ashamed buff on the pawn, gossip and reputation among witnesses, and a bouncer or staff reaction in shops and venues.
- **Variety:** reactions vary (48: never the same line twice in a row), and the same act lands differently by context. A kiss at a bar on a flirty stranger is not the same as a kiss at the courthouse on a stranger with a partner.
- **Sometimes it works:** the existing scored outcomes still happen when the fit is close enough, and success odds fall smoothly with the gap.

Parts 2–5 of 52 (knowledge topics, NPC replies in context, curating every object and place by what's possible, and the sweep) stand. In part 4, "hide what can't be done" means practically impossible, not socially unwise. The UI-shot note about dropping the "(Acquaintance)" header label and the home entries with no pet, TV or radio also stands.
