**12 city infrastructure: utilities, transport, emergency services, government, public spaces and planning are in (branch agent/city12, one commit per section 12.1 to 12.5/12.6).** Full notes in `docs/CITY.md`.

- **Utilities (12.1):** the town is cut into 4 zones; power, water and phone/internet are real networks with outages (storm, overload, faults, burst mains), boil water notices, generator / solar panels / water tank / well, and every bill is split by utility (electricity, water, phone and internet, council charges, cover). Mailbox: Check Utilities, Report an Outage, Boil Drinking Water.
- **Transport (12.2):** cars (buy, fuel or charge, park with limited spaces and a car park, insure through the existing car policy, crash, tow and pound), bikes, 3 bus routes with a timetable, taxis, traffic by hour, traffic lights (dark when the zone has no power), road works and road quality. Commutes and the player's lot trips choose a way by time, cost and weather.
- **Emergency services (12.3):** response time from the station lot to the call (distance, traffic, staffing from the budget, units already out, no phone line). Fire engines, ambulances (the patient waits, a long wait makes things worse) and patrol cars use it; far patients feel it.
- **Government (12.4):** taxes and a budget that really fund utilities, fire, police, ambulances, roads, parks and schools; a townie mayor and council; elections every 3 game years with campaigns, speeches, a politician career for a player who runs, votes driven by the town mood; laws (curfew, park hours, quiet hours, free buses, green grants, permits).
- **Public spaces and growth (12.5 / 12.6):** street lamps that break and are fixed, bins and litter, benches / toilets / fountains / playgrounds, holiday decoration, ageing pipes; zoning, and projects that take days (scaffolding, workers, opening) which lift the population cap, revenue or budgets.

**Not done / honest limits:** no 3D for any of it (no car, bus, engine, scaffolding or decoration models; people just cross the map faster); the map does not grow (no free slot for new blocks, a fire station or a city hall: the fire station shares the police lot, city hall is the courthouse lot); no dedicated UI screens (mailbox interactions, toasts, the household dialog shows utilities and the bill split, and the `city` command); sewage is only part of the water network (no sewer backup event); townies are statistical for everything here.

**Shared files touched (small hooks):** Economy (compute_bill/issue_bill via UtilityBills), Interaction.start (`ride_minutes`), Commute / Workplace / NeighborhoodLots (ride choice), Hospital (`emergency`) with Health*/incident_effects callers, FireBrigade, Police (respond, patrol_factor), CrimeRate (budget and lamps), Venues.check_entry (laws), CareerShifts (school budget), StoryGrowth (population cap), SystemsManager / SimRegistry / SaveLoad / share_schema (`systems.city`), CommandActions (`city`), tests/sim_scheduler_test.gd (three new minute rows), tests/baseline/coop_hash_3day.json (regenerated on purpose: new city block, mayor and budget are part of the sim).
