61d, TownDrama (scripts/systems/town_drama.gd, data/town_drama.json)
- **When:** at world creation (new_game.gd, after the townies and the solo start).
- **How many:** 4-8 threads, scaled by adult townies / 40, with the scale floored at 0.8 so a small town still gets a handful. Clamped to 3-10.
- **Rolled from the world seed:** the number, the kinds, the people (each person in one thread at most) and the depth. Two games don't share a set.
- **Kinds,** each a real state in an existing system:
  - feud: negative history both ways plus a JusticeState grudge with rage and a start date in the past. Never within a family or one home;
  - affair: someone with an exclusive partner, romance with another, and an entry in RelationshipService.affairs, so the cheating discovery takes it from there;
  - secret: a real secret of A's that B has learned (RelationshipKnowledge), usable for gossip and blackmail;
  - debt: an overdue loan from a townie loan shark (CrimeUnderRacket, one payment already missed). Skipped if the town has no shark;
  - crew: CrimeLifePath member with some cred, 40% on probation;
  - cold case: a burglary case in state cold from 1-5 months ago, with the real culprit living in town;
  - business: a townie's business with negative cash, bad days, low rep and stars;
  - family: an estranged parent and adult child, or a divorce (flags and Family.spouses cleared);
  - addiction: the existing addiction state, 0.3-0.6, started weeks ago;
  - tension: two single people with some romance and a flirt in their history.
- **Weights:** by kind, and when the lead person lives in a rough district, crew x2.5, debt x2, addiction x1.6. Never by heritage.
- **Nothing scripted afterwards.** No beats, no meeting, no storyteller push; the systems run it. Nothing shows the threads to the player. A "TOWN DRAMA kind: A / B" debug line goes to stdout only.
- **The end-of-life summary:** once a day, a thread the player is now part of (friendship >= 30 with someone in it, or a grudge >= 0.2) goes into the LifeLedger once ("Got to know Petra Eames, in the middle of the feud between Tobias Alcott and Petra Eames."). "drama" is also a cause kind for a later jail, separation, revenge, forgiveness or civil entry, so the summary can link them.
