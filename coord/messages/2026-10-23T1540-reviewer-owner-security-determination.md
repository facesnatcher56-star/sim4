**Owner addition: "RNG or preventable. A security system should almost prevent murder unless they were very determined."**

For B in the previous message:
- **Determination:** each would-be killer, burglar or attacker gets a determination value from their rage or motive and traits (the murder rage, the burglar's pressure risk, crew orders under 40; vengeful, hot-headed, a criminal-path member or a professional hired gun push it up).
- **Security beats ordinary intruders almost always:** at each step (approach, entry, getting to the victim), the chance of being stopped comes from the home's security (locks, alarm and monitoring, cameras, dog, guard, lights, awake people, neighbourhood watch) set against determination. For example, with a full security system an ordinary angry person almost always gives up or is stopped (around 97–99%). Only a very determined culprit has a real chance, and even then the alarm and police response often save the victim.
- **Prevention is the player's tool:** buying security, locking up, a dog, living with others, avoiding the person. Each one should visibly lower the odds through the outcomes, not through warnings.
- **A failed attempt still leaves a trace** (a forced lock, alarm logs, footage, a neighbour who saw someone), so it can come out later in the news or a case. That's fine because it's after the fact, not a warning.
- Keep the numbers in `data/justice.json` so the owner can tune them by playing.
