**06: Natural disasters and utilities.**

These are town-scope events that hit the whole open map, with a **forecast warning** where it's realistic (the forecast already exists). Very rare: think once in several playthroughs. Damage scales with how prepared the lot is.

- **Power outage** (the most common, about 0.5 times a year, more in storms or blizzards or if the bills are unpaid): electronics, lights and heating stop, the fridge food spoils faster, and it's dark at night.
- **Blizzard:** heavy snow, folk can't travel, heating bills go up, exposure risk.
- **Tornado** (spring or summer, about 0.02 times a year): a warning siren, then objects outside get destroyed or thrown, roofs get damaged, and anyone caught outside can be injured or rarely killed. Hiding indoors or in the basement or bathroom keeps you safe.
- **Flood** (after long heavy rain, about 0.02 times a year): ground-floor tiles get wet, floor-level objects are damaged, mould comes afterwards.
- **Earthquake** (about 0.01 times a year, no warning): objects fall and break, there are injuries, and walls can crack.
- **Wildfire** in a dry, hot summer near the park (about 0.005 times a year): smoke over the town, and it can spread to a lot.
- **Hailstorm:** breaks outdoor objects and crops.
- **Meteor strike or other freak events** (about 0.0005 times a year, as an easter egg): destroys a random outdoor spot. It's harmless unless someone is standing there.
- **Insurance:** an optional weekly cost added to the bills that pays back part of the damage.
- **Aftermath:** cleanup interactions, repair costs, the town helping (neighbours visit and give gifts), and shared grief if townies died.
