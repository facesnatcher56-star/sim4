**09b: How crimes get discovered.** This is the owner's design and adds to 09. Crimes don't need a witness to be found out. **Nothing goes unnoticed forever**, just like real life.

**1. Discovery.** Every crime is either *witnessed* (reported straight away) or *hidden*. A hidden crime is found later:
- **Missing person:** the victim stops showing up at work or school, misses bills, stops answering friends, misses a date. Each missed thing raises a "missing" score. Family, partner, coworkers or close friends report them missing once it crosses a threshold. The delay depends on how connected the victim was: hours for a family member, days for a worker, possibly weeks for a loner with no job.
- **Body found:** someone passes the place, a neighbour notices a smell, or a search party (non-graphic: a notification and a short scene, no body shown).
- **Theft noticed:** the owner sees items or coins missing the next time they look, or at the next bill.
- **Fire or damage** is obvious straight away.

**2. Investigation after discovery.** Police look for **connections**: who last saw the victim, grudges and relationship history, money motives (inheritance, debts), alibis (where everyone was, which the sim already knows), stolen items turning up, and gossip. Then the case goes through 09 (arrest, trial). Cases with weak connections can stay open, but **police keep checking**: new evidence or gossip can reopen them, and the Chronicle tracks open cases.

**3. Serial killer (one of the rarest events in the game).**
- An extremely rare townie archetype (around 0.001 per game year per town, so most towns never get one). They pick **isolated targets**: loners, people without jobs, few friends, out alone at night.
- **Disappearances are noticed slowly** because the victims have few connections, but the system spots a **pattern**: several missing isolated people in one area within a short time leads to a special investigation, town-wide fear ("Uneasy at night", folk staying home after dark), newspaper headlines, and more police on the streets.
- They are **eventually caught**: every crime raises the chance of a mistake, a witness or a link. The longest run is a handful of victims before arrest, then a major trial.
- Keep it non-graphic and low-detail. It's background town drama the player mostly hears about through news and rumours, unless their own Folk are involved.

Tests: a hidden murder of a family member is reported within about a game day, a jobless loner within a week or so, and a theft is noticed when the owner next checks. A forced serial-killer run gets caught within the victim cap, and the pattern detection fires.
