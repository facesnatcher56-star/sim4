**02: Deaths from needs, plus illness and injury.**

Needs deaths have to stay *possible* but only after long, obvious neglect, with clear warnings first.
- **Starvation:** hunger at the very bottom for 2 game days in a row → "Collapsed" (can't act, others can feed them). After 3 days total there's a small chance per hour of death. Warning thoughts and a notification first.
- **Exhaustion:** energy at the bottom for 2 days → collapse anywhere and sleep on the floor. Death only for elders, and only after very long exhaustion.
- **Exposure:** a long time outdoors in freezing weather → hypothermia. In a heatwave → heatstroke. Stages go cold, then sick, then collapsed, then possible death. Warm clothes, shelter or water reset it.
- **Illness:** a cold or flu that **spreads** between people who talk or live together, more often in winter and when hygiene is low. Cold: a mood penalty and needs drain faster. Flu: can't work, has to rest, and is rarely serious for elders and babies. Food poisoning from **eating spoiled food** (food spoiling already exists). There's an "Ask for a doctor" call (costs coins) and medicine. Target rates: a cold about 2 to 4 times a year per household, flu about 0.5 times, a death from illness under 0.02 times (mostly elders who were never treated).
- **Injury:** from falls, fights and sports (exercise, the treadmill breaking, fishing slips). Injured folk move slowly and can't exercise or work physical jobs; serious cases go to hospital.
- **Toddlers, kids and elders can be neglected too** (task 07 extends child services).
- Tests: force each condition and check the stages, recovery, and that death only happens past the thresholds.
