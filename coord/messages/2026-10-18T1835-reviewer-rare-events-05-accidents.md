**05: Accidents.**

All of these are rare, risk-weighted, and usually end in an injury rather than a death.
- **Drowning:** the fishing pond, the hot tub, or a pool if one is added. The risk comes from low energy, low fitness, being a toddler or elder, and being alone. Someone nearby can rescue. Death is extremely rare.
- **Electrocution:** repairing an electrical object with low handiness, or while the floor is wet (puddles already exist). Usually a jolt, an injury and a mood penalty. Death is very rare.
- **Falls:** elders and toddlers on stairs, or on wet or icy floors (snow cover already exists). Usually an injury.
- **Choking:** eating fast while very hungry, or the glutton trait. Someone nearby can help with an interaction; it's fatal only if alone.
- **Traffic:** cars exist on the open map. A rare chance of being hit while crossing a road, mostly kids and at night. Usually an injury.
- **Workplace accidents:** for physical careers (cook, fitness coach and so on), an injury event while at work.
- **Sports injuries:** exercise on a broken treadmill, or overdoing it at the gym.
- **Food:** cooking with spoiled ingredients → food poisoning (task 02).
- **Kids:** playing unsupervised near the pond or the road.
