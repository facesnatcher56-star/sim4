**23: An open moral sandbox: be anyone, good or bad.** From the owner: the player's folk can choose any life (saint, hero, cop, doctor, thief, killer, hitman, dealer, crime boss), and the world reacts. It extends 21. Same rules as always: **game mechanics only, abstracted** (skills, rolls, contracts, interactions, events; no real-world methods, recipes or instructions), non-graphic, follow 17's rules, townies run it statistically, and every crime flows into discovery (09b), charges and sentencing (20), and reputation.

**Out of scope, do not build:** human trafficking, or any buying, selling or exploitation of people, in any form (playable or background). Also no sexual content of any kind in crime systems.

**1. Underworld careers** (hidden careers with ranks, contacts and reputation, joined via contacts, a record or desperation)
- **Hitman:**
  - **Contracts:** come from crime bosses or desperate townies with grudges (09c revenge), and have a target, a fee, a deadline and conditions.
  - **The job:** research the target's routine (where they go, when they're alone), pick an opportunity, and the kill is an abstracted event using 04's murder flow (non-graphic).
  - **Risks:** witnesses, cameras (21), police, and the target's family's revenge.
  - **The other side of it:** contracts can also be **refused, botched, or tipped off** (warn the target for a reward). Townies can take out contracts on the player's folk too (very rare, needs a strong grudge).
- **Drug dealer (fictional substances only):**
  - **The trade:** buy from a supplier contact, sell to townies with an `addiction` state (02 health: cravings, mood swings, money drain, overdose risk, rehab at the clinic), and run territory or corners.
  - **Risks:** undercover stings, rival dealers, informants, raids with a search warrant.
  - **Effects on the town:** dealing raises the town crime rate (09c) and addiction among townies.
  - **Production stays abstract:** "a supplier" or "a stash", with no recipes or processes.
- **Smuggler or fence:** contraband (fictional goods, stolen items) moved through the market, the docks or the cruise ship (22); customs checks.
- **Racketeering:** loan sharking (high interest, "collection" visits that pressure debtors, 09c pressure), illegal gambling dens (13), protection rackets on businesses (pay or suffer vandalism), counterfeit cash (abstracted), and money laundering through a front business (13).
- **Crime families and gangs:** organisations with a boss, ranks, territory, loyalty and betrayal; gang wars between them (rare, the town crime rate spikes); recruitment of the player's folk; rising to boss; an informant or witness-protection path.

**2. The good side, just as deep**
- **Police officer and detective** (09): patrols, real cases from the town (including the player's criminal rivals), stings, undercover work in a gang, corruption offers (take the bribe or not).
- **Prosecutor and defence lawyer** (09), **judge**, **firefighter** (03), **paramedic and doctor** (02, 10), **social worker** (20 child services), **security guard** (21).
- **Vigilante or hero:** stop crimes in progress (with risks), rescue people (fires, drownings), a town reputation as a hero.
- **Redemption:** a reformed criminal can go straight (a mentor, a job that hires people with records, expungement), while old contacts may pull them back.

**3. Reputation and consequences**
- **Two reputations:** public (town) and underworld. Both move with actions and are visible to those who'd know.
- **Moral memories:** guilt and pride moods based on traits (a "ruthless" or "remorseless" trait shrugs off guilt; an empath suffers).
- **Family reactions:** they find out (secrets), disown, cover up, or join. The legacy score has notoriety and honour sides.
- **The world reacts:** police attention rises with heat, shops refuse a known thief, victims' families seek revenge (09c), and the Chronicle and newspaper report the crime waves and the busts.

**Tests:**
- A full hitman contract flow (accept, research, execute, discovery, and investigation reaching the player at a rate that rises with mistakes).
- A dealer gets caught in a sting at the expected rate.
- An addicted townie's money and health decline and rehab works.
- A gang war raises the crime rate, and it decays afterwards.
- A cop accepting a bribe gets an internal-affairs risk.
- A redemption path clears the record over time.
- The town stays mostly peaceful when the player is a good citizen (the background crime rates from 19 point 3 still hold).
