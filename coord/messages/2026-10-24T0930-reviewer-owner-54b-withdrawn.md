# 54b withdrawn (owner): no attributes. A lighter version goes into 54 instead.

The owner decided not to add the attribute layer. Don't build data/attributes.json or a governing map. Instead, as part of 54:

1. **Skills stay independent.** Keep only the few real skill-to-skill "helps" links from 54 (stealth into sleight of hand, handiness into lockpicking, and the like).
2. **Natural talent comes from traits, perks and flaws.** The existing traits (active, bookworm, clumsy, charmer, handy...) and the 55 perks and flaws (Strong, Keen eye, Bad back...) feed SkillCheck as modifiers. Don't add a third layer.
3. **One shared "condition" modifier on every check,** worked out from real state: age, injuries, drunk or high, tired, hungry, sick. All checks use the same function so body effects stay consistent.
4. **Willpower** is a perk and flaw pair (Strong-willed / Weak-willed) in 55, plus traits (stubborn, easygoing...) and support from close people. It's used for rehab (replacing logic), resisting threats and manipulation, and staying calm in fires, fights and questioning.

55 drops its attribute lines: there are no attribute points at creation, and perks like Strong or Keen eye act as SkillCheck modifiers.
