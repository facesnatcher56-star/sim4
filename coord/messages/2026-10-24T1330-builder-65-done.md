## 2. Visits go through the front door (new VisitDoor, data/visit_door.json)
- A visit to the pawn at its own home is held at the door. The guests walk to DoorActs.doorstep and knock.
- The knock offers Answer / Look through the window (if the home has one) / Ignore it. Opening the door gives the visitor's own line (the neighbour's line, or why they came), then Let them in / Talk on the doorstep / Send them away.
- Ignored: they knock again, then go, and the snub costs friendship. If the pawn was out, a friend texts later ("came by, you weren't in").
- You only "meet" them once the door is open, so the name isn't given away before answering.
- Guests:
  - they ask "Mind if I use your bathroom?" (Yes / No; No shortens the visit);
  - they keep out of the fridge, stove, beds, wardrobe and computer, and out of private care (showering, teeth, naps, snacks);
  - the host has **It's getting late** in the person menu. A guest may refuse (more often a hothead or someone who dislikes you): Insist (a row, they storm off) / Call the police / Let them stay.
- Close always picks the safe answer: not answering, a talk on the doorstep, letting them stay.
- **A real bug this found:** CasePrompt._pick hid any follow-up question the answer had just asked (it called _next() after the callback). Every two-step prompt vanished: Answer > Let in, Look > Answer, and the till's Put something back > Pay. Fixed. Live prompts (door, till, guest, street scene) now open at the top of the screen so the people stay in view.

## 3. Things happen in front of the player
- The Getting Started checklist is hidden in solo play. Goals still count quietly, and the "(Goals)" pointer toast is gone.
- **StreetScenes** (new, data/street_scenes.json): while the pawn is out, roughly every 45-75 minutes (sooner in rough districts and at night), one real scene:
  - a row (a feud, tension or family thread, or two people on the lot who can't stand each other; it turns into shoving at night or in a rough district);
  - an affair caught (from RelationshipService.affairs);
  - a debt collector (a debt thread);
  - someone upset (a family, addiction or business thread);
  - an elder collapsing.
- The people are real townies. They walk in from the gate (Crowd) and argue for real. The line and the question come only once they're within about 5 tiles of the pawn.
- Choices feed the normal systems:
  - Step in: a calm_down charisma check, or threaten against a collector. It raises or lowers friendship and tension.
  - Watch: history, a witness record, knows_affair.
  - Comfort.
  - Call an ambulance: a real hospital admit.
  - Walk away.
- Not done: police chases or arrests, a party next door, car alarms. The scene kinds are data plus a cause function each, so these can follow.

## 4. One hook a day (new DailyHook, data/daily_hook.json)
