**12: City infrastructure and services.** From the owner: expand every other area of the game and the town's infrastructure as deep as the crime and justice work. This is the first of three expansion tasks (12, 13, 14).

**Queue:** after everything already queued (02 to 11 and 09c), unless a piece is needed earlier by those. Use multi-floor (10) and commutes (11) wherever it makes sense. Same rules as always: data-driven, works for townies (full when near, statistical when far), chronicle hook and notify policy, long-run tests.

- **Utilities as real networks:**
  - **Power:** a grid with an outage source (storms, unpaid bills, overload).
  - **Water and sewage:** burst mains, a boil-water notice.
  - **Internet and phone service:** needed for phones, computers and remote work.
  - **Bills:** each lot's bills are split by utility. Solar panels, a generator, a water tank and a well are buyable objects that cut bills and add resilience.
- **Transport:**
  - Usable cars (buy, fuel or charge, park, insure, crash, tow) with **parking** on lots and in the garage.
  - **Buses** on routes with stops and a timetable, plus taxis and bikes.
  - Traffic density by time of day, traffic lights at junctions, and road works.
  - Commutes (11) choose a mode by cost, time and weather.
- **Emergency services as real places:**
  - A **fire station** whose trucks drive to fires (03).
  - **Ambulances** from the hospital (10).
  - **Police** cars and patrols (04/09).
  - Response time depends on distance, traffic and the town budget.
- **Government:**
  - A **city hall** building with a **mayor** and council (townies, or a player Folk via a politics career).
  - **Elections** every few game years, with campaigns, speeches (the speech skill exists) and votes driven by town mood.
  - **Taxes and a town budget** spent on police, fire, roads, parks and schools. The budget changes response times, crime rate (09c), road quality and school quality.
  - **Local laws and policies** voted in (curfew, park hours, noise rules), plus permits for building and business.
- **Town planning and growth** (extends 08):
  - **Zoning** for residential, commercial and civic land. New blocks open as the town grows: the map grows outward with new roads, lots and buildings.
  - **Construction happens over days:** scaffolding, then the building appears, with workers.
  - **Ageing infrastructure:** potholes and broken streetlights that the budget fixes.
- **Public spaces:** streetlamps (they affect crime and night fear), benches, bins (litter, if they aren't emptied), public toilets, fountains and playgrounds, and seasonal decoration on holidays.
