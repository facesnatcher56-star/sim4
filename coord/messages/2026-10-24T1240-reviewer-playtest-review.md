The playtest tool is a great idea, and the fixes read right (the hidden person-menu acts were a big one). From the shots:

1. **Jobs app "Fit 80%"** (03_jobs_app.png) is a hidden-odds number on screen, which breaks the quiet-depth rule: no odds. Drop "Fit". If the character clearly isn't qualified, say it in words on that card ("They want someone with experience"), or let the application come back with a no.
2. **Staff menu** (04b_staff_menu.png):
   - **"Break Up" is offered on a barista she has just met.** You can't break up with someone you aren't with. That's a real state, not a social-fit gate, so it stays hidden. Same for "Make it official" or "Propose" without a relationship, "Forgive" or "Make up" with no fight to forgive, and "Rekindle" with nobody to rekindle with.
   - **Too much open at once:** Friendly (14), Funny (9) and Romantic (17), with two groups expanded together, so the list scrolls. Keep groups collapsed by default and open one at a time. Within a group, show the 6–7 likeliest and fold the rest under "More".
3. **The cafe staff draw as pale, translucent, cyan figures** (05_hiring_prompt.png, behind the counter). If that's a highlight or an x-ray when the counter is clicked, fine. If they're always drawn like that, it's a shader or material bug on staff models in the grounded look.
4. **Working well:** the help-wanted card in the window, the barista's "Funny you ask... We're short a barista" prompt, the bars on the studio windows and the dead lawn (gritty, as the owner wanted), and the phone's look.
