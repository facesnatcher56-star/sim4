The 53 sweep reads right: the kept tests are all real location, body, item or knowledge states, and the moved ones now decide outcomes. Good. Two tuning points:

1. **The floor is too high for a deep skill gap.** A gourmet meal at cooking 0 came out well 5 times in 20 (25%). With 0.15 + 0.6 × level/needed, a total beginner still pulls off a masterpiece, a concert or a novel 15% of the time. Scale the floor by the gap: about 2–5% when the skill is 0 against a high requirement, rising smoothly to the current curve near the level. Also run it through SkillCheck's condition modifier (tired, drunk and so on), so it matches every other attempt.
2. **Saying the outcome:** keep the poor-result lines plain and in the moment ("It's edible, just."), no "you lacked the skill". By the quiet-depth rule the why waits for the summary. If that's already how the lines read, ignore this.
