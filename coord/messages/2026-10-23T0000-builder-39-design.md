Task 39 design (with the owner's "emergent, not forced" amendment applied) is on main: docs/CHALLENGES_PLAN.md (docs only, no game code; I will not build it until the owner decides). Summary:
1. Measured with a throwaway storyteller-only probe (5 seeds x 60 days, one player household, no hourly incident dice): 70 to 80 percent of cards land elsewhere in town; personal bad cards are only 0.4 a week on Classic, 0.55 on Chaotic and 0.7 on Custom-max; the longest gap with nothing personal is 17 days on Chaotic despite the 3-day quiet guard, because the guard counts district ripples and 60 percent of its plays go to a district card.
2. Why hits don't hurt: coins can't go negative, there are no job reviews, arcs run on timers whose default answer is the soft option, choices have no prices and nothing is remembered, the family secret ends in a toast, and the card `wealth` tilts act as the "knock back the player who is doing well" the amendment forbids.
3. Approach: about ten threads, each with hidden meters that grow only by their own cause; a step fires when a meter crosses a threshold, not on a timer; every ask has priced options and one ignore outcome, and the answer or the silence goes into a saved ledger. StorytellerArcs grows into this engine (meters, when, option costs and needs, wait_days, ignore) with a new ledger panel.
4. No steering: wealth tilts are ignored for the pawn; eviction_scare is replaced by the real rent ladder; burglary weight follows visible valuables and locks, not the balance; pawn_focus and the personal budget only add seeds to the player's circle; the weekly medium floor is gone.
5. Money and work: rent ladder (reminder, missed rent, notice, 3-day deadline, bailiffs and lock-out; notice after 3 missed rents on Easy, 2 Normal, 1 Hard); debt (friend favours, bank refusal, a loan-shark path built on CrimeUnderRacket, which needs a player-debtor path; offered after the notice on Normal and from the first missed rent on Hard); job at risk (review, probation, rival), layoffs, sick-call and medical-debt asks off the existing health and hospital code.
6. Arcs: feud, partner strain, partner tempted (mature switch), wrongly accused, rival, admirer (safety first), friend in trouble, a habit (off by default); the family secret ends in a sibling, a debt claim, or a conditional inheritance.
7. Things that stay: a missing-objects list after a break-in, damaged rooms after a fire, hospital instalments, reputation effects (greetings, job offers, landlord and lender terms, refused service), community service versus shifts; jail of 2 days or less counts as missed shifts instead of an instant quit.
8. Difficulty: Easy softens the endings (couch, relief grant, legal aid); Hard hardens them (street, collateral and a bruise, real jail days); the hard rarity rules stay.
9. Test (observations only): pawn_bot_threads_longrun_test on Normal and Hard, 5 seeds x 60 days, careful bot and ignore bot, printing per player-week threads in motion, threads that grew, lowest coins as a share of weekly costs, near-loss days; asserts only that threads exist and step by their meters, the ignore bot loses its home by about day 21 to 28, the careful bot keeps its home in at least 4 of 5 seeds on Normal, a steer-free test (50 versus 5,000 coins gives the same card sequence), and determinism and save/load.
