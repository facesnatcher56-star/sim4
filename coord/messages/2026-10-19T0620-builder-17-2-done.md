**Stage 1, `SimScheduler`:** the only Clock listener (`scripts/sim/sim_scheduler.gd`; one instance per Clock). A tick runs the minute layer, then on the hour the hour layer, then at midnight the day layer, each in (order, seq), preserving the old signal order. Job kinds: plain periodic (cadence 1, 5, 15, 60, 1440 with a phase and an optional emptiness gate: an idle gated job costs one call), bucketed (`add_bucketed` plus `job.track(id)`: each id is visited once per cadence at minute `id % cadence`, nothing tracked costs zero), and timed wake-ups (`at(minute, owner, fn, args)`, fired in (minute, seq) order). CRITICAL and NORMAL never defer; LOW defers once the frame's sim work passes `budget_usec` (0 = unlimited by default; `main.gd` sets 4000), with postponed work run first later or forced after 12 ticks and counted in `stats()`. `scripts/sim/sim_registry.gd` is the table: adding a clock hook is one row; every SystemsManager minute, hour and day step, SimDirector, SocialDynamics, ScenarioGoals, Main's autosave and Vacation (gated row) are migrated; no `*_passed.connect` remains in sim, systems, net or core except the scheduler (a test enforces it; view-only listeners such as hud and lot_view stay on the Clock signals). `SimScheduler.describe()` generates the registry table into `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` ("The scheduler").
