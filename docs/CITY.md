# City infrastructure (task 12)

Data: `data/city.json` (zones, utilities, later the budget and the council), `data/incidents/12_city.json` (buffs, objects, interactions), `data/transport.json`, `data/services.json`.
State: ONE save block `systems.city` (`CityState.to_dict`, listed in `data/share_schema.json`). `CityState` owns the seeded dice (`CityState.rng`, saved) and the zone helpers.
Rules every city system follows: the player's household(s) are FULL (real objects, buffs, bills), townie lots are STATISTICAL (one-line lot events, no per-person work),
nothing loops all folk / all objects in a clock hook, every number is data, every player action is a command, every long-run rate has a slow test.

## 12.1 Utilities as real networks
- **Zones**: the town is cut into districts (`zones`). A household belongs to a zone by its id (`CityState.zone_of_household`), a townie lot by its number.
- **Outages** (`Utilities`): `begin(util, zone, hours, cause)`; `util` is `power`, `water` or `internet` (phone and internet). Causes are data (`utilities.causes`): power = storm / overload / fault, water = burst main / pump fault, internet = line fault. Hourly dice per zone and cause (`UtilityEvents`): rate per zone-year x weather multiplier x maintenance (`CityBudget.maintenance_mult`), repairs take hours / `CityBudget.repair_speed`.
- **Boil water notice** (`Utilities.start_boil`): after a burst main (45 percent), by contamination (x8 during a flood). People of the zone get the `boil_notice` buff; unboiled tap water can make them ill (daily dice); `boil_water` (kettle / stove) makes a batch safe for 12 hours; a well household is not affected.
- **At home** (`UtilityHomes`): service = the zone's service plus the home's own backup. Generator (fuel burns hourly), solar panels (power only in daylight), water tank (a reserve of hours, refills when water is back), well (water without mains, needs power for the pump). Markers: `power_out`, `no_water`, `no_signal` (phone), `offline` (internet; also when the home has no power); interactions in `needs_water` / `needs_signal` / `needs_online` refuse while the marker is on (`UtilityContent`, same patch idea as `DisasterContent`). Lamps go dark and come back; a warm fridge spoils (reuses the disaster outage numbers).
- **Unpaid bills**: phone and internet are cut after a missed bill (`overdue.internet_after_missed`); the existing `utility_cutoff` incident still cuts the power.
- **Old town-wide power cut** (`power_outage` incident, 06) is also an outage of the power network in every zone (`Utilities.external_power`); its own handler still treats the homes.
- **Bills split by utility** (`UtilityBills`): fixed charges by `fixed_share`, use by the utility the interaction used, `cover` = insurance premiums. Solar panels (-35 percent each of the power part), a water tank (-20 percent), a well (-55 percent). `Economy.compute_bill` is the sum; the bill record carries `parts`, the toast and `UtilityBills.describe` name them.
- **Player**: mailbox interactions `Check Utilities` (plain-word status per utility with the reason and the time left), `Report an Outage` (crews 25 percent quicker, once per outage, needs a phone line); `Boil Drinking Water` at the kettle or stove. Toasts follow the household policy (only the player's own zone); the chronicle gets outages of 3 hours or more and every water outage.
- Tier behaviour and cadence: see the headers of `utilities.gd` / `utility_homes.gd`; the clock rows are `Utilities` (minute, gated), `hour/Utilities.on_hour`, `day/Utilities.on_day`.
- Tests: `tests/city_utilities_test.gd` (fast), `tests/city_utilities_longrun_test.gd` (slow: rates per cause, thunderstorms, a starved budget).

## 12.2 Transport
Data: `data/transport.json`. Code: `Transport` (state + API), `TransportCars`, `TransportParking`, `TransportBus`, `TransportTraffic`, `TransportTrip`, `TransportCrash`, `CityCmd` (commands).
- **Cars** (household assets, up to 3): `TransportCars.buy/sell/refuel/repair/collect`. Models in data (petrol and electric; an electric car charges cheaper on the home lot). Car insurance is the existing car policy of `BankInsurance` (weekly premium on the bill, pays 70 percent of repairs). Bikes are per household. All are player commands through the command layer (`Commands.run({action: "city", args: {action: "buy_car" ...}})`) and mailbox interactions (Buy a Car: ..., Fill Up the Car, Repair the Car, Collect the Car from the Pound, Sell the Car, Buy a Bike, Check Bus Times).
- **Trips** (`TransportTrip.plan/start`): for commutes and for the player's lot trips (own vehicles only there). Candidate ways: on foot, bike (weather: none in snow or storms), own car (parked at the start lot, has fuel, adult or elder driver), bus (timetable wait + ride + walk to the stops + fare), taxi (needs a working phone line; base + per tile; longer wait at night). Score = minutes + cost x value of time (a poor household values coins x2.5) with a wet-weather penalty for exposed ways. Commutes use the best way for the lead time (`Workplace.trip_minutes`, cached 10 minutes) and `Commute._walk` hands `ride_minutes` to the walking interaction (`Interaction.start`), so the person crosses the map faster. Rides shorter than 24 tiles are walks.
- **Parking** (`TransportParking`): spaces per lot type minus the share other people take at that hour (offices fill in the working day); a full lot means a search, the car park (daily fee) or the street. A car on a venue street overnight is towed with a chance per night (pound fee grows each day, `Collect the Car from the Pound`); a daytime ticket is possible. A car on its own drive is never towed.
- **Crashes** (`TransportCrash`): chance per trip x night x rush hour x weather x tired driver x road quality x broken traffic lights x worn car. Minor (dent), serious (hurt, repairs), wreck (towed, hospital, rare death). Serious ones go to the chronicle. Bikes can fall off.
- **Buses** (`TransportBus`): three routes with ordered stops, headway by band (rush hour 8, day 12, evening 20, night 60 minutes), one change at a shared stop, exact timetable from the game minute (no bus bodies in the world). The transport budget stretches the headway.
- **Traffic** (`TransportTraffic`): density by hour (rush hours), weekends, weather; traffic lights at the junctions of the street grid (walk / stop phases; with the zone's power out the lights are dark, drivers slow down and crossing is more dangerous: `Traffic.risk_at` multiplies by `tile_mult`); road works slow a zone; roads age (faster in the cold) and the budget starts resurfacing where they are poor; a burst water main puts a pipe crew in the street. Potholes (very poor roads) raise crash chance and car wear.
- Tier behaviour: only the players' households own vehicles; townies stay statistical (ambient parking and the traffic curve). Rows: `Transport` (minute, gated on trips in progress), `day/Transport.on_day`.
- Tests: `tests/city_transport_test.gd`, `tests/city_commute_test.gd` (real map), `tests/city_transport_longrun_test.gd` (slow).

## 12.3 Emergency services as real places
Data: `data/services.json`. Code: `CityServices` (stations, response time, units out), `CityAmbulance` (the ambulance hold).
- **Stations are lots of the map**: the fire station is its own lot (`fire_station`, east side), police have the public-safety building, ambulances start at the hospital lot. Units are drawn driving to the call by the vehicle layer (task 12b).
- **Response time** = call handling (1-2 min) + turnout (crew in the station; staffing follows `CityBudget.funding(dept)`) + the drive (Manhattan distance between the lots, siren speed 4, traffic eased by `traffic_effect`, dark lights and road works through `TransportTraffic.speed_mult`) + 8 minutes when every unit is already out + 6 when the caller has no phone line. `CityServices.dispatch` rolls the dice (seeded) and sends a unit out until it is back.
- **Fire**: `FireBrigade.call_in` takes the delay from the engine's response (a smoke alarm still saves 2 minutes); the arrival toast says how long it took. **Police**: `Police.respond` uses the patrol car's response for crimes in progress. **Ambulance**: `Hospital.admit` for severity 2+ on the real map first waits for the ambulance (`CityAmbulance.hold`: toast with the expected minutes, then the admission); a wait of more than 18 minutes can make the condition one step worse. Without a map (test worlds) and for townies nothing changes.
- **Far people**: statistical hospital stays of townies get their death chance times `CityServices.death_mult()` (1.0 at the standard budget, up to x2 when the ambulance service is starved).
- Tests: `tests/city_services_test.gd` (real map), `tests/city_services_longrun_test.gd` (slow: average responses over every lot and hour, starved budgets).

## 12.4 Government: taxes, budget, mayor, council, elections, policies
Data: `data/city.json` `gov`. Code: `CityGov` (budget, taxes, mayor, council, save), `CityMood`, `CityElection`, `CityPolicy`. Everything is optional: a player who never touches it still lives in a town that has a mayor, a budget and a council.
- **Budget**: `CityGov.tax` (0.6 .. 1.6) scales the council-charges part of every bill (`UtilityBills`) and the town's weekly revenue (a base + per head + per player household). Shares (`alloc`: utilities, fire, police, health, roads, parks, schools, reserve) divide it. `funding(dept)` = share / default share x revenue / standard revenue (1.0 = standard), topped up from the savings (25 percent a week) and cut by 20 percent in a debt. `CityBudget.funding` reads it, so the budget really changes: utility breakdown rates and repair speed, bus headway, road decay and works, response times of fire / ambulance / police, school grade gains, the crime rate (`CrimeRate.components.budget`).
- **Mood** (`CityMood`): services (response vs targets), utility outage hours, taxes, crime, roads -> -1 .. +1, `CityMood.words()` for the player, `complaint()` names the worst thing.
- **Mayor and council**: the first mayor and four council members come from the townies when the game starts (`CityGov.ensure`). City hall is the courthouse lot (the civic centre): the politician career works there.
- **Elections** every term (84 days = 3 game years): two weeks before, the candidates are fixed (the mayor if willing, townies with charisma, any player who chose `Run for Mayor`); candidates gain support daily and from `Give a Campaign Speech` (podium; charisma matters, three speeches a day count). On election day townies vote with a score of charisma + support + a platform that answers the town's complaint + a bonus for the mayor in a happy town and a penalty in an angry one (`Vote for Mayor` for the household during the campaign). A player who wins gets the `mayor` buff and the politician career at Mayor level; a townie mayor applies their platform (taxes and shares).
- **Policies** (`CityPolicy`, data): curfew for under-18s (venue entry at night, fewer street crimes), park closing hours, quiet hours (noise fines), free buses, green energy grants (bigger solar cut), permits (a building permit per day, a business permit). The council votes by leaning (axes: safety, freedom, thrift, services, peace, green); a law cannot be reopened for 28 days; townie mayors bring one about monthly; a player mayor uses mailbox interactions (Council: ..., Raise / Lower Taxes, Budget: more for ...).
- Shared-file hooks: `Venues.check_entry` (policy), `Police.patrol_factor`, `CrimeRate.components`, `CareerShifts` school gain, `TransportBus` fares, `UtilityBills`, `BusinessCmd.open` + `CommandActions._build_wall` (permits), `SystemsManager` interaction start (noise), `SysContent.install_all` (`CityGov.install`).
- Tests: `tests/city_gov_test.gd`, `tests/city_gov_longrun_test.gd` (slow: incumbent win rate by mood, 30 years of budgets).

## 12.5 Public spaces and ageing infrastructure
Data: `data/city.json` `public`. Code: `CityPublic`.
- **Street lamps** per zone (8): they break daily (more as the infrastructure ages), crews fix two a week times the parks funding; dark zones count against the crime rate (`CrimeRate.components.lamps` uses `CityPublic.lamp_equiv`) and against the town's mood.
- **Bins and litter**: bins fill; the parks budget empties them weekly; full bins turn into litter (toast for the player's zone when it gets bad).
- **Benches, public toilets, fountains, playgrounds, bins**: counts per zone; the parks budget (funding 1.05 or better) and the savings build one every eight weeks. Objects with the same names exist as content (`streetlamp`, `bench`, `public_bin`, `public_toilet`, `fountain`, `playground`, with `toss_coin` and `play_on_playground`) so they can be placed on a lot; the town's own are numbers.
- **Holiday decoration** for three days (announced, a little mood), **ageing**: the infrastructure age rises slowly and is renewed by utilities funding (>= 0.6); `CityBudget.maintenance_mult("utilities")` grows with it.

## 12.6 Town planning and growth
Data: `data/city.json` `plan`. Code: `CityPlan`.
- **Zoning** (residential 55 / commercial 30 / civic 15 percent by default); the mayor shifts it (`Rezone: more ... land`).
- **Growth**: weekly, a town that is 90 percent full starts a project (a block of flats, a row of shops or a community centre) in a zone chosen by the zoning shares, if the savings pay for it. **Construction takes days**: scaffolding (2-4 days), then 3-8 workers build (8-14 days), then it opens (toast for the player's zone, chronicle).
- **Effects**: flats raise the population cap (+4: `StoryGrowth` arrivals and births use `max_population + CityPlan.extra_capacity()`), shops raise the town's revenue, a community centre adds to the parks and schools budgets.
- **Land** (task 12b): every project takes one of five reserved plots on the east side (`map_layout.json` `plots`); scaffolding, a rising building site and the finished building are drawn (`PlotView`); no free plot, no new project. City hall (`city_hall`) and the car park (`car_park`) are real lots too.
- The town notice (mailbox: `Read the Town Notice`, `CityGov.status_lines`) shows mayor, mood, taxes, where the money goes, laws, elections, projects and dark lamps in plain words.
- Tests: `tests/city_town_test.gd`, `tests/city_town_longrun_test.gd` (slow).
